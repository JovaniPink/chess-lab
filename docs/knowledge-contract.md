# Public knowledge projection

Chess Lab exports its bounded public study at `/knowledge.json` using `knowledge-contract.v1`.

The projection connects the exact PGN, reconstructed positions, legal-move exercises, human-authored analysis notes, and a retrospective. The PGN digest fixes the game snapshot used by every object. Existing study and lesson identifiers remain aliases.

This record demonstrates traceability, replay, and exercise mechanics for one personal game. It does not claim rating improvement, training effectiveness, engine evaluation, or a representative sample. Imported PGNs and training plans remain session-only and never enter the public projection.
